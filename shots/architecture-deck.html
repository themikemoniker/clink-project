CLINK is five specs.

21001Offersnoffer Ask a node for an invoice. This is the payment.
21002Debitsndebit Spend from a node, under a cap the node sets. Our refunds.
21003Managenmanage Create and edit offers — from a browser holding no key.
21004Enroll— Provision an account on a node. Spec'd, not yet built.
30078Beacon— How a node announces itself and its fees.

Three of them carry this demo. Every one is a signed event over a public relay.

The node is Lightning.Pub.

✓21001 Offers — every payment in this demo
✓21002 Debits — the refund, capped by the node itself
✓21003 Manage — how every offer here was minted
✗21004 Enroll — not implemented in v0.0.37
✗21000 Not CLINK at all — Lightning.Pub's own RPC, keyed on a secret a remote signer never exposes. A browser cannot speak it.

That last line is why Manage exists in the flow — not as a nicety, but because nothing else could reach the node.
