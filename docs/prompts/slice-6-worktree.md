### What a worktree does NOT isolate

This is the part that can actually break the demo:

- **One Lightning.Pub node, one account.** Both agents' `check-manage.ts` runs mint
  real offers onto the same account, and `--clean` deletes across both. If the other
  agent is mid-verification and you clean, you delete their fixture.
- **Four public relays.** Kinds 30402, 30405, 15128 and 10063 are all replaceable —
  last `created_at` wins, globally. Two agents seeding, publishing or deploying are
  overwriting each other's events on the open internet, not in a branch.
- **One kind 15128 per pubkey** (`5A.md:16`, findings §13.22). A deploy from your
  worktree using a key the other agent is also using **replaces their site**, silently.
- **Four Blossom servers**, and `~/.lamppost-key-backup/`.
- **The live demo storefront**, at the npub in `/docs/status.md`. It works. Neither
  agent should redeploy it without asking.

⇒ **Ask me before anything that writes to the node, the relays, or Blossom.** That is
`seed-listings.ts`, `mint-offers.ts`, `deploy-nsite.ts`, `authorize-manage.ts`,
`check-manage.ts` without `--dry`, and the builder's Publish and Deploy buttons.
Reading is free: `check-buy.ts` (the decline path), `check-deploy.ts`,
`deploy-nsite.ts --dry`, `mint-offers.ts --dry`, and every `npm test`.

### Branch discipline

- Commit to `slice-6` only. Never commit on `main` from the worktree, and never
  `git checkout main` there — a worktree cannot check out a branch another worktree
  has, and trying is how you end up in a detached HEAD wondering where your work went.
- The other agent's fixes will land on `main` underneath you. **Rebase onto `main`
  rather than merging it in**, and do it early and often rather than once at the end —
  slice 6 and a review pass both edit `/builder/src`, and a five-day-old rebase across
  a refactor is a bad afternoon.
- If a rebase conflicts inside something the review agent rewrote, **take their
  version and re-apply your change on top.** They are fixing; you are adding. Do not
  resolve a conflict by reverting their fix, and if their fix and your slice genuinely
  disagree about how something should work, stop and ask me rather than picking.
- `/docs/spec.md`, `/docs/status.md` and `/docs/spike-findings.md` are the files most
  likely to conflict, because both of you are appending to them. Append at the end of
  a section rather than rewriting the section, and keep your doc edits in the same
  commit as the code they describe so a conflict is legible.
- When you are done: `git worktree remove ../clink-slice6` only after the branch is
  merged or I have said otherwise. Do not delete the directory by hand — that leaves
  a stale worktree registration.

## The slice

**`/docs/prompts/slice-6.md` is the brief.** It is committed, it is current, and it
names the three problems underneath "edit, restock, mark sold, view settled sales,
private notes". Do not re-derive them. The headline, so you know what you are walking
into:

**"View settled sales" may have no CLINK path at all.** CLINK Manage's only resource
is `"offer"` (`clink-manage.md:29`) — create, update, get, list, delete. There is no
invoice or settlement resource. Settled sales live behind `GetUserOfferInvoices`,
which is a kind 21000 native RPC, which a NIP-46 bunker cannot speak (findings
§13.18). So a browser admin panel behind a Signer **cannot see the seller's sales**.
Verify that against the spec file yourself, then decide what the bullet becomes. It
is an architectural consequence of holding no key, not a missing feature.

The other two: an edit is a re-cut ladder and possibly a re-minted offer, with a
window where a stale rung republishes old text over new; and the fixture's five
offers were minted natively so CLINK Manage cannot see them (findings §13.20).

## Gotchas that will cost an hour each

`/docs/status.md` has the full list. On top of the worktree ones above:

- **A NIP-46 bunker cannot speak kind 21000.** In the browser it is CLINK or nothing.
  Reaching for `pub-rpc.ts` in `/builder` means you took a wrong turn (findings §13.18).
- **Manage `list` does not show natively-minted offers.** An empty list on an account
  with seven is not a bug (findings §13.20).
- **CLINK Manage `create` is not idempotent** (`clink-manage.md:226`). N requests, N
  offers. `update` MUST NOT add new fields (`clink-manage.md:193`).
- **Never delete a depleted offer.** It takes the buyer's stored refund pointer with
  it — `GetUserOfferInvoices` is the only reader and it throws once the row is gone
  (findings §13.17). This is also the "mark sold" decision slice 6 owes.
- **`authorize_npub` wants a HEX pubkey despite the name.**
- **The three CLINK error envelopes differ.** One parser for all three is a bug.
- **`pool.subscribeMany` takes a single filter OBJECT** in nostr-tools 2.24.3.
- **`verifyEvent()` caches its verdict on the event object** (findings §13.10).
- **The gateway caches for an hour.** Verify deploys against the relay and Blossom,
  never against the gateway. `node spike/check-deploy.ts <npub>` does it in that order.
- **Never guess a CLINK kind, field, tag, or error code.** They are in
  `/docs/clink-notes.md` with citations. Write `UNVERIFIED` and ask.
